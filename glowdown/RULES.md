# Glowdown

Be the first to play out every card in your hand. Two to eight players; bots fill empty seats.

## The cards
- Four suits of light: **Sun**, **Moon**, **Star** and **Bolt**. Each has its own shape on the card as well as its own colour.
- Every suit has the numbers **0 to 9**, twice.
- Every suit has three action cards, twice each:
  - **ZAP +2**: the next player draws two cards and misses their turn.
  - **BLOCK** (⊘): the next player misses their turn.
  - **BOUNCE** (⇄): play turns round and goes the other way. With two players it works like BLOCK.
- Eight **PRISMs** belong to no suit. Play one on anything and name the suit to play next. Four of them are **PRISM +4**: the next player also draws four and misses their turn.

## How to play
- Everyone gets seven cards. One card is turned up to start the pile.
- On your turn, play a card that matches the pile: the **same suit** as the suit in play, or the **same face** (a 7 on a 7, a BLOCK on a BLOCK). A PRISM always plays.
- Can't play, or don't want to? Tap the pile to **draw** one card. If it plays, you may play it straight away or **PASS**.
- The first player to play their last card wins.

## House rules
The host switches these on or off before the first game; they are all off to begin with. **PLAY AGAIN** keeps them. At the end, the host's screen shows the next game's rules: tap **CHANGE** to switch them for the next game. Playing alone against bots, the game starts straight away with the rules you last played.
- **Stack draws:** answer a ZAP +2 with another ZAP +2, or with a PRISM +4; answer a PRISM +4 only with another PRISM +4. The draws add up, and the first player who can't answer draws them all and misses their turn.
- **Jump in:** if you hold the exact card on top of the pile (same suit, same face), you may play it even when it isn't your turn. Play goes on from you.
- **7 swaps, 0 rotates:** play a 7 and swap your whole hand with any player you pick. Play a 0 and every hand moves on one seat, the way play is going.
- **Draw until you can play:** when you draw, keep drawing until you get a card that plays. You may then play it or pass.

## Scoring
- The first player out wins.
- Everyone else is placed by the points left in their hand, fewest first: numbers count their face value, action cards 20 and PRISMs 50.
- Whoever played first goes round the table each new game.

## Example
The pile shows a Moon 4. You hold a Sun 4, a Moon BLOCK and a PRISM. The Sun 4 plays (same face) and so does the Moon BLOCK (same suit). Ben next to you has one card left, so you play the BLOCK and he misses his turn. With **Stack draws** on, Ben could have answered a ZAP +2 with his own ZAP +2, and the player after him would draw four.

## Controls
- Tap a card in your hand to play it. Cards that can play now are raised and outlined.
- Tap **DRAW** to draw. After drawing a card that plays, play it or tap **PASS**.
- For a PRISM, pick the suit. For a 7 with **7 swaps** on, pick who to swap with.
