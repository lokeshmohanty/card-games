# Glowdown

Be the first to play out every card in your hand. Two to eight players; bots fill empty seats.

## The cards
Glowdown has a deck of its own: 60 cards.
- **Five suits** from the night sky: **Nova**, **Comet**, **Crystal**, **Orbit** and **Eclipse**. Each has its own picture on the card as well as its own colour.
- Every suit has the numbers **1 to 8**, once each.
- Every suit has three action cards, once each:
  - **SPARK +1**: the next player draws one card and misses their turn.
  - **ECHO** (≈): play again straight away, a card of the same suit or a PRISM, or **PASS**.
  - **GIFT** (⇥): give one card from your hand to the next player.
- Five **PRISMs** belong to no suit. Play one on anything and name the suit to play next. Two of them are **PRISM +3**: the next player also draws three and misses their turn.

## How to play
- Everyone gets five cards. One number card is turned up to start the pile.
- On your turn, play a card that matches the pile: the **same suit** as the suit in play, or the **same face** (a 6 on a 6, a GIFT on a GIFT). A PRISM always plays.
- Can't play, or don't want to? Tap the pile to **draw** one card. If it plays, you may play it straight away or **PASS**.
- Play always goes the same way round the table.
- The first player to play (or give away) their last card wins.

## House rules
The host switches these on or off before the first game; they are all off to begin with. **PLAY AGAIN** keeps them. At the end, the host's screen shows the next game's rules: tap **CHANGE** to switch them for the next game. Playing alone against bots, the game starts straight away with the rules you last played.
- **Stack sparks:** answer a SPARK +1 with another SPARK +1, or with a PRISM +3; answer a PRISM +3 only with another PRISM +3. The draws add up, and the first player who can't answer draws them all and misses their turn.
- **Snap:** if you hold the same number as the top card, in any suit, you may play it even when it isn't your turn. Play goes on from you.
- **Prisms pass hands:** when a PRISM (not a PRISM +3) is played, every hand moves on one seat, the way play goes.
- **Draw until you can play:** when you draw, keep drawing until you get a card that plays. You may then play it or pass.

## Scoring
- The first player out wins.
- Everyone else is placed by how many cards they still hold, fewest first. Players holding the same number share the place.
- A player who leaves the game isn't placed.
- Whoever played first goes round the table each new game.

## Example
The pile shows a Comet 4. You hold a Comet ECHO, a Comet 7 and an Orbit 4. You play the ECHO (same suit) and go again: the Comet 7 follows. Ben next to you has two cards left. On your next turn you play a GIFT and hand him your Orbit 4, which leaves you one card. With **Snap** on, anyone holding a 4 could have snapped it in when the Comet 4 was turned up.

## Controls
- Tap a card in your hand to play it. Cards that can play now are raised and outlined.
- Tap **DRAW** to draw. After drawing a card that plays, play it or tap **PASS**. After an ECHO, play another card of the same suit or tap **PASS**.
- For a PRISM, pick the suit. For a GIFT, pick the card to give.
