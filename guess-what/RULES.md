# Guess What

Everyone sees a picture except the guesser, who has to work out what it is from the others' clues before the time runs out. Two to eight players; bots give clues and guess too.

## Every phone (the party game)
- Players take turns to be the **guesser**. Everyone guesses the same number of times.
- In each round every other phone shows **the picture** and its name. The guesser's phone shows **nine pictures** from the same category, and one of them is the answer.
- The others give **clues**. Say them out loud, or type them into the clue box so every phone sees them, which works even when you are not in the same room. A clue may not use the picture's name or any other name for it.
- The guesser taps the picture they think it is. A wrong tap crosses that picture out and costs **5 seconds**.
- The round ends with the right tap, or when the clock runs out. Then everyone sees the answer.

## Scoring (every phone)
- **Right:** the guesser scores **3**, or **4** if more than half the time was left. Every clue-giver scores **1**, and whoever typed the last clue before the right tap scores **1 more**.
- **Time up:** nobody scores.
- After the last round, the most points wins. Players on equal points share the place.

## One phone
Play on one phone by adding people at SINGLE PLAYER's table as **PERSON** seats (bot seats sit out).
- On your turn, look away, or hold the phone to your forehead with the screen facing out. Someone taps **GO**.
- The others describe each picture without saying its name. When you say it, they tap **GOT IT** and the next picture comes up. **PASS** skips a picture.
- Get as many as you can before the clock runs out. Each one is a point.

## Before you start
The host picks:
- the **categories**: Animals, Food, Things, Sport & play, Travel & places, Nature & sky;
- the **seconds** a turn: 30, 45 or 60;
- the **turns** each player has: 1, 2 or 3.

Pictures don't repeat on this phone until every picture in the chosen categories has come up. Who guesses first goes round the table each new game.

## Example
Asha is guessing; the picture is a giraffe. Ben types "long neck" and Cara says "spots". Asha's nine animals include a zebra, a leopard and a giraffe. She taps the leopard: it is crossed out and she loses 5 seconds. She taps the giraffe with 30 of 45 seconds left and scores 4. Ben and Cara score 1 each, and Ben, who typed the last clue, scores 1 more.

## Controls
- **Clue-givers:** type in the box and tap **SEND**, or press Enter.
- **Guesser:** tap a picture.
- **One phone:** **GO**, **GOT IT**, **PASS**, and **NEXT** between turns.
